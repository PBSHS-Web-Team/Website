Korean Language Program

The Korean Language Program helps students develop basic to intermediate skills in reading, writing, listening, and speaking the Korean language. It also introduces Korean culture, traditions, and workplace communication to prepare students for future opportunities related to international studies and employment.

Chance to Work in Korea

Students who complete the Korean Language Program may gain opportunities to apply for training, scholarship programs, or employment pathways connected to South Korea, depending on qualifications and available partner programs. The program aims to enhance communication skills, discipline, and global competitiveness.

Grade 11 (Senior High School K-11)

Grade 11 focuses on building foundational knowledge, critical thinking, communication skills, and career preparation. Students are introduced to specialized subjects based on their chosen strand while participating in activities that promote leadership, collaboration, and personal growth.

Grade 12 (Senior High School K-12)

Grade 12 prepares students for college, employment, entrepreneurship, or further technical training. Students strengthen their academic and practical skills through advanced subjects, performance tasks, and real-world learning experiences aligned with their chosen strand and future career goals.
