The Student Council of Palawan Bethel Senior High School serves as the voice of the student body and promotes leadership, responsibility, cooperation, and active participation within the school community. The council organizes school activities, supports student programs, and helps strengthen unity among learners through service-oriented leadership and meaningful initiatives. Together, the officers work hand in hand with the school administration, teachers, and students to create a positive, disciplined, and engaging learning environment that encourages academic excellence, creativity, and student empowerment.
