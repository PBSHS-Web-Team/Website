Christian Living

Through Bible studies, worship activities, prayer gatherings, outreach programs, and values formation, students are guided to build a deeper relationship with God while applying Christian principles in their daily lives. The school promotes a Christ-centered environment where students are encouraged to become responsible individuals and positive contributors to their families, communities, and society.

Christian Living also inspires students to develop leadership, humility, service, and perseverance as they prepare for their future goals and personal growth. By combining faith, education, and character formation, the program aims to nurture well-rounded individuals who can make a meaningful difference in the world.
